Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Chess Coach does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your games, rating and settings stay on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your current or last game, with the coach’s comments.
  • Your rating and your game history.
  • Your settings, such as the opponent, the options and the theme.
  • In the iPhone app, whether Pro is unlocked (a copy of what the App Store says).

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. The iPhone app also keeps a backup copy in its own settings storage on the device, so that iOS can’t clear your rating when the device runs low on space. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

If you back up your iPhone (with iCloud Backup or to a computer), that backup may include the app’s data, under Apple’s terms. We never see it.

What the app sends over the internet

  • The iPhone app makes no connections of its own. The chess engine and everything else it needs are inside the app, and it works in airplane mode. iOS contacts Apple’s App Store for the app to show the price of Pro, to buy or restore it, and to keep your purchase up to date (for example after a refund).
  • The web version (atg-y2k.github.io/chess) is downloaded from GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub. Like any website, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. After that, the web version works offline and sends nothing.
  • Links to other websites (such as the source code or these pages) open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. The app asks Apple’s StoreKit, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it uses no cookies (the web version keeps its data in your browser’s storage on your device). On the App Store, its privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

iPhone: delete the app. This removes its data from the device (an older device backup may still contain it until it is replaced). Pro stays with your Apple Account: after you install the app again, tap Restore Purchases in the Menu. Web: clear the website data for atg-y2k.github.io in your browser’s settings. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.
