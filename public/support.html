How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Menu and tap Restore Purchases (in the Chess Coach Pro section), or tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. You never pay twice: buying again with an account that already owns Pro just unlocks it.

I bought Pro, but it’s still locked

  • Tap Restore Purchases in the Menu.
  • Check that this iPhone uses the Apple Account that bought Pro: Settings → your name → Media & Purchases.
  • If your family uses Ask to Buy, Pro unlocks as soon as the request is approved. The Menu says “Waiting for approval” until then.
  • If it still doesn’t unlock, contact us.

Does Family Sharing work?

Yes. Pro is shared with up to five other members of your Family Sharing group, when you share your purchases with them. They tap Restore Purchases to unlock it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund, Pro is locked again.

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

Always free: every opponent at any level, your rating and game history, the evaluation bar and graph, the rating of each move (Best, Mistake, Blunder…), also of the computer’s moves if you like, and your accuracy, takebacks and Retry, and PGN export.

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds the coach’s explanations of why each move is good or bad (yours and the computer’s), Hints, Show best, the explorer (try moves before you play them), best-move arrows, and the full Game Review with its key moments and a comment on every move.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The chess engine and everything else are inside the app, so it works in airplane mode from the first launch. Pro stays unlocked offline too. The App Store is only needed to buy or restore Pro.

Where are my games and rating?

Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, so your games and rating don’t move to another device, and deleting the app deletes them. The free web version keeps its own, separate data. See the Privacy Policy.

The engine doesn’t start or seems slow

  1. Close the app completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.
  2. Run the engine self-test: Menu → Engine → Run engine self-test. It starts the chess engine, runs a few searches and shows PASS or FAIL.
  3. Tap Copy log and paste the log into an email to us.

On iPhones with less memory, the app may switch to one shared engine (“compatibility mode”) for 14 days. The coach then pauses while your opponent thinks. Try two engines again in the Menu’s Engine section turns it off.

What do the ratings mean?

Your rating is an Elo rating against the app’s computer opponents, from 100 to 3200. It goes up when you do better than expected against a bot and down when you do worse. These are engine-scale ratings: they are not FIDE or online-site ratings, and they won’t match them exactly. A game where you use a hint, a takeback, Retry, best-move arrows, the explorer or Rate opponent’s moves doesn’t count for your rating.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later.

Is it open source?

Yes. Chess Coach is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later, and uses the open-source Stockfish chess engine. The source code of every release is at github.com/atg-y2k/chess. The app’s Menu → About shows the licenses and credits.
